A Message From Your Loved One ❤️

The happiest day of my life was on 12/05/2022; little did I know it would be the day our story began. 🤍 All I remember is this cute/handsome guy, dressed in navy, sitting across from me in Qahwa 30 mins late. You made me feel a little shy, and I remember thinking what a decent guy I see, how well-mannered he is. After our second date, I went home and told my mum, "He's definitely going to reject me. I don't think he's interested at all." Looking back now, it's funny how wrong I was. Then we met once more before you travelled, and that's when my happily ever after quietly began. 🤍🤍

We started talking day and night. I was falling for you so deeply🤍🤍, but I don't think I truly understood the depth of what I was feeling until 6th July, when you called me and told me you wanted to take serious steps. Back then I still didn't fully understand what I was feeling, though, that's when it started the 🦋🦋. That phone call changed my life forever. Then came 02/10, my graduation day, the moment I saw you there, something inside shifted. It told me that one day we would be exactly where we are today.

One thing that will always amaze me is that the day we first met, 12/05, became the very same day we had our Katb Ketab💍. As if it was our destiny from the very beginning. And now, as if that wasn't enough, our little one 👶 decided to join us on that same day to make it more special. Every time I think about it, I can't help but smile and wonder how incredibly blessed and lucky I am. It reminds me of the beautiful verse: " وَمِنْ آيَاتِهِ أَنْ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنْ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَزْوَاجًا لِّتَسْكُنُوا إِلَيْهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيْنَكُم مَّوَدَّةً وَرَحْمَةً."

The more I grow with you, the more I truly believe in love stories. 🤍🤍 Sometimes I think about the beautiful idea that a wife was created from her husband's rib, not just to be above him or below him, but close to his heart, to protect it and to be protected by it. Thinking of us makes that idea feel so real, and it reminds me that you were always written for me, long before either of us even knew it.

You have filled my life with happiness, peace, and a love I never knew existed. I always used to say that I would never get married or even travel alone with another person. I genuinely believed that. But then I met you. Because you are not just another person. You are my family, my best friend, my safe place, my biggest supporter, my comfort, my home, and most importantly, my lifetime partner. 🤍🤍🤍🤍 Every single day, I see how you go above and beyond just to make me smile, to make me feel loved, and to make my life better. I notice every little thing you do, and I will never stop being grateful for it. I only hope that, in my own way, I can always give you even a small glimpse of the happiness, comfort, and love that you give me every single day. This little website is just a tiny reflection of what I see when I look at you. Hehe, there is no project of Shahd's....

Thank you for choosing me. ❤️

Thank you for loving me. ❤️

Thank you for making all the ordinary moments feel extraordinary. ❤️

And thank you for being the greatest blessing I have ever received. I love you more than words could ever describe. Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️❤️
